# Data Ethics and Data Governance — Pontificia Universidad Javeriana

*Spanish version: [README.md](README.md)*

Two projects from the *Research Seminar*, MSc in Data Science, Universidad Santo Tomás (2026), applied to the same organization: Pontificia Universidad Javeriana (Bogotá).

**Team:** Adrian Grateron · Jhonatan Smith Doncel · Jorge Emmanuel Marín

## 1. Ethics audit of an organization

Ethical and regulatory assessment of personal-data processing under the university's data policy and Colombia's **Law 1581 of 2012** (personal data protection):

- Informed-consent evaluation and risk level.
- Inventory and classification of sensitive data, legal basis and security measures.
- Potentially discriminatory variables (explicit and proxies) with a risk matrix.
- Internal governance policies and data-subject rights (channels, response times, complaints).
- International data transfers.
- Overall risk matrix, recommendations and implementation timeline.

## 2. Data governance framework

Data governance proposal for the university's academic-administrative system, based on **DAMA-DMBOK**:

- Governance roles and a **RACI** matrix.
- Data quality dimensions and metrics.
- Data catalog and master data (academic structure, administrative roles, employee contract lifecycle).
- Role-based access policies and request/approval workflow.
- Anonymization, pseudonymization and masking protocols.

*Academic work; not an official audit and does not represent the position of Pontificia Universidad Javeriana.*
